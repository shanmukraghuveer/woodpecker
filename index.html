About The Artisan

Driven by Passion & Perseverance

Welcome to my atelier. My name is R. Ashok Kumar, and I am an artist who truly enjoys the work. For me, woodworking is a lifelong passion and a personal hobby. Shaping raw timber into intricate sculptures, wall reliefs, and models is something I deeply love doing every single day.

Even when space was limited in my home workspace, I never stopped creating. Throughout time, I faced space constraints and technical challenges, but I conquered every problem through patience, practice, and pure dedication to my craft.

🛠️ Artisan Scope & Privacy Notice

I specialize strictly in fine hand-carved crafts, wooden decor, wall reliefs, and small accent furniture (side tables, stools, trays, boxes). I do NOT undertake industrial manufacturing, commercial bulk orders, or mass-produced millwork. All orders are handled 100% online/remotely—the home atelier is closed to public in-person visits.
